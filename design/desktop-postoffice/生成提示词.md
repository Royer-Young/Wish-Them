```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Transform the attached “暖木愿望邮局” mobile concept sheet into ONE polished desktop Web application screen for “愿TA”. The attachment is the approved visual identity reference, not an image whose mobile layout should be retained. Preserve its original mascot 小愿: chestnut-brown hair with two short side braids, envelope-shaped pale-gold hairclip, cream blouse, rust-red pinafore dress, brown shoes, small canvas postal satchel; friendly chibi pixel girl. It must be unmistakably a desktop practical app with a cozy 16-bit videogame atmosphere and generous horizontal composition.
COMMON DESKTOP SYSTEM: a slim horizontal header about 72px, left pixel envelope logo and “愿TA”, central navigation exactly “邮局首页 / 今日 / 愿签池 / 账本 / 我的”, right notifications envelope and small round paper avatar. Clearly indicate only the active navigation item. All non-hero content sits on quiet cream paper, generous desktop gutters 40-64px, 24px between regions. Deep brown readable simplified Chinese text, restrained characterful pixel-style headings, highly legible sans-serif Chinese body copy. Palette paper #FFF1C8, lighter reading surface #FFFAED, wood #C88B46, main action #B94735 with cream text, sage #607254, dark ink #392B27. Stepped 2px outlines, subtle 3-4px offset shadows, crisp pixel clusters. Full illustration should look like refined deliberately drawn 16-bit pixel art, not smooth vector, not anime painting, not blurred pixelation. Typography can be cleanly typeset for usability. Do not impose a pixel font on long text or tiny data. Keep wood texture off reading surfaces.
PRODUCT TRUTHS: practical care app, making a wish for another person, two mandatory daily tasks (为TA and 为自己), optional real money saving. No fake currency, no shop, no XP/combat/rankings. Photos and questions private by default; explicit public sharing only. Today's page shows today's completion only, never journey day index, total days or countdown. Gift identity and gift illustration appear only after second settlement act; before that an unobtrusive CLOSED plain wooden drawer with a gold “?” is allowed, never a wrapped present. All buttons describe real actions. 小愿 occupies a purposeful small region or scene, never blocks a form or data. No watermark, no extraneous labels, no generic corporate SaaS charts. Exact short Chinese labels should be accurate. Design a complete composition with ample breathing room, realistic desktop app hierarchy, not many repeated decorative panels.
SCREEN: the atmospheric POST OFFICE HOMEPAGE, “邮局首页” navigation active.
Make a memorable panorama: the interior of a warm wood post office opens onto a beautiful sunset canal through a very large right-hand window; floating wish notes on the water, red mailbox, warm hanging lamp, wall shelves, small plant, writing desk. The signature is a desk connected to the canal outside. Hero occupies about 70% of the viewport height, NOT a tall mobile poster.
LEFT 42% of hero is an uncluttered large cream paper sign integrated with the wooden room, with a small line “暖木愿望邮局”, large exact headline over two lines “有些话说不出口，\n就替TA许个愿。” supporting line “把心意写下来，从今天的一件小事开始。” A clearly dominant large rust-red text button “替TA许个愿” and secondary paper button “看看大家的愿望”. Small lock caption “愿签默认私密，由你决定是否分享”. Do not put an extra title inside a boxed dashboard.
RIGHT 58% is the lovely detailed pixel scene; 小愿 standing behind the postal counter holding a wish envelope, enough scale to read her face (about 180-230px high), warm smile, not gigantic. A little pixel speech bubble “这句话，我替你收好了。” A single preview wish note on the desk reads “替妈妈许愿 / 希望妈妈每天都睡得好一点”.
BOTTOM integrated calm cream strip about 140px high, three small horizontal explanatory cues with pixel icons and readable labels “写下心愿” / “做今天的小事” / “把心意带到现实”. It is process orientation, not stats or marketing cards. Decorative plants and wood remain at the edges. Cohesive warm amber lighting, beautiful generous space, usable website with strong pixel personality.
```
