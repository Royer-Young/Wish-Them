# 愿TA 像素视觉方案生成提示词

本次使用内置图像生成工具。以下为三张视觉概念板的完整提示词；用于设计探索，不是可直接切图上线的生产素材。

## 暖木愿望邮局

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished high-resolution presentation board for an ORIGINAL Chinese practical mini program called “愿TA”. Desired output one wide 2560x2048 board, front-on flat UI, not a photograph, no perspective, no real device hardware. Design coherent original 16-bit cozy pixel art UI: deliberate sharp pixel clusters, stepped corners, dark colored outlines, 2-tone sprite shadows, integer-looking pixel scale, no blur, no glossy 3D, no gradients or vector-anime illustrations. Take inspiration from cozy pixel videogame interiors with warm furniture, expressive chibi girls and chunky cream dialogue boxes; do not reproduce any existing character or watermark.
PRODUCT: users make a wish for a specific other person, do two daily tasks (one caring for TA and one for oneself), optionally save/earn real money, then receive a two-act ending and a tangible gift suggestion. This is a practical app with game atmosphere. Prioritize large readable simplified Chinese, visible text buttons, clear input fields, spacious paper surfaces. Original full-body girl must be consistently SAME across scene, character portrait and expression strip; 2.5-head chibi proportions, fully clothed, friendly, non-sexualized.
BOARD COMPOSITION: elegant header with “愿TA” and the supplied Chinese concept title; LEFT about 77% of board contains SIX complete narrow 375:812 mobile UI panels in 3 columns x 2 rows with short captions, RIGHT about 23% has a large original mascot, her name, six distinct small pose sprites in 2x3 grid, five labeled color swatches, three pixel component examples. Neutral matching paper background around panels, generous gutters. Board is a professional design proposal, not a game screenshot collage. Panels have small mini-program capsule at upper right, safe areas, sufficient bottom navigation room. 4-tab bottom nav where appropriate with labels “今日 / 愿签池 / 账本 / 我的”. Text headers can use pixel-style Chinese but body copy must be clear Chinese sans serif.
SIX SCREENS EXACT CONTENT AND RULES:
1 首页 — “愿TA”, headline “有些话说不出口，就替TA许个愿。” Large atmospheric pixel scene showing the mascot and wish notes on water, large main button “替TA许个愿”, secondary “看看大家的愿望”.
2 创建愿签 — title “替谁许愿”, relationship chips “妈妈 / 爸爸 / 姥姥 / 朋友”, selected “妈妈”, a paper input “希望妈妈每天都睡得好一点”, visible optional control “AI润色”, a distinct compact “AI辅助” label on suggestions, primary “下一步”. Show caring conversational mascot portrait but do not use chat as main navigation.
3 今日 — title “今天的小事”, two mandatory paper task cards with category and action: “为TA · 打个电话” and “为自己 · 好好吃饭”; task buttons “去记录”; optional small third card “可选 · 记一笔省下的钱”; “已完成 1/2”. Important NO day-of-journey number, NO “第3天”, NO countdown, no total days on this screen. A tiny gold closed “?” remains mysterious with no gift-name spoiler.
4 记录 — title “留住这一小步”, big image-upload slot with pixel camera icon and “添加照片或截图”, optional note field “写一句话（选填）”, privacy caption “照片仅自己可见”, button “完成记录”.
5 账本 — title “小小账本”, simple real-money entries “省下 ¥48”, “挣到 ¥120”, prominent correct total “合计 ¥168”, small “记一笔” button, closed gold “?”; NO gift names or imagery before reveal. No coins as fake currency, no shop, no microtransactions.
6 两幕结算 — show paper wish with readable stamped “已还愿”, then below “彩蛋已开启”, real-money total “你攒下了 ¥168”, a gentle real-world gift icon and editable letter, buttons “保存心意” and “分享愿签”. Only here show gift suggestion “一份给妈妈的心意”. No medical promise, no guaranteed wish magically true.
MASCOT EXPRESSION POSES labels: “招手 / 思考 / 记录 / 开心 / 陪伴 / 递信”. Beside poses show small pixel dialogue box “慢慢来，我陪你。”
Component samples: primary button, selected relationship chip, checked task box. Design clear hierarchy and readable short copy; avoid filler text, watermark, fake brands, combat/health bars/XP/rankings, guilt-inducing sad girl, unreadable tiny UI.
CONCEPT A TITLE “01 暖木愿望邮局”. Most faithful to the user's warm amber pixel-video atmosphere. Colors: paper cream #FFF1C8, honey wood #C88B46, vermilion #B94735, muted sage #607254, ink brown #392B27. Dense pixel-art set dressing ONLY in scene areas, clean cream paper on forms. Motifs: sunset post office, wooden shelves and counters, red postbox, envelopes, red wax seals, small canal outside the window. Homepage combines a warm wooden post office and water with floating paper wishes. Cards resemble stationery resting on a wooden notice board. UI borders pixel stepped 2px, 4px offset brown shadows, primary cinnabar rectangular buttons.
ORIGINAL MASCOT “小愿”: chestnut-brown bob with two tiny short braids, an envelope-shaped gold hairpin, cream blouse, rust-red pinafore dress, dark brown shoes, a small canvas postal satchel. She is a warm reliable young postal helper, not a maid, no animal ears, no tail. Full-body front, readable face, main action holding a folded wish note; one smaller rear view beside her. Six chibi pixel expressions retain identical outfit and silhouette. Story metaphor: wishes are letters, daily tasks are notes being delivered, final seal reveals a tangible act of care. Make this a cohesive charming pixel tool with excellent everyday usability.
```
